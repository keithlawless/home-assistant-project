# Taco cheat sheet

Start every request with **"Hey Taco"**, then speak normally. You don't need
exact wording: the phrases below are examples. The ones marked ⚡ are handled
instantly on the Pi. Everything else goes to Taco's AI assistant, which takes a
second or two longer but understands far more.

If Taco mishears, just say "Hey Taco" again and repeat yourself.

## The basics

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Stop" · "Never mind" · "Thanks" ⚡ | Stops talking, silences a ringing alarm, or stops the music |
| "What time is it?" · "What's the date?" ⚡ | Tells you |
| "Turn it up" · "Quieter" · "Volume 6" · "Set the volume to 40 percent" ⚡ | Changes Taco's volume |
| "Stop listening" · "Mute the mic" ⚡ | Turns the microphone off. Tap **Mic off** on the screen to turn it back on |
| "Cleaning mode" · "Clean the screen" ⚡ | Ignores the touchscreen for 30 seconds so you can wipe it. A countdown shows at the top. Voice still works |
| "Stop cleaning mode" · "I'm done cleaning" ⚡ | Turns the touchscreen back on early |

## Timers, alarms and reminders

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Set a timer for 10 minutes" ⚡ | Starts a timer |
| "Set a pasta timer for 12 minutes" ⚡ | Starts a named timer (handy when you have more than one) |
| "How much time is left?" · "How long on the pasta?" ⚡ | Tells you what's left |
| "Add 2 minutes to the pasta timer" · "Add 5 more minutes" ⚡ | Extends a timer |
| "Pause the timer" · "Resume the timer" ⚡ | Pauses or resumes |
| "Cancel the pasta timer" · "Cancel all timers" ⚡ | Cancels |
| "What timers are running?" ⚡ | Lists timers and alarms |
| "Set an alarm for 7:30" · "Wake me up at 6 tomorrow" ⚡ | Sets an alarm |
| "Remind me to call Mom in 20 minutes" · "Remind me at 5 pm to start dinner" ⚡ | Sets a reminder that Taco speaks aloud |

When a timer or alarm rings, say **"Hey Taco, stop"** or tap the screen.
If you have several timers and Taco asks "Which one?", just answer with the
name, for example "pasta".

## Weather

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "What's the weather?" · "How cold is it outside?" ⚡ | Current conditions at home |
| "How warm is it inside?" · "What's the humidity in the house?" ⚡ | Indoor readings, if you have a weather station |
| "Will it rain tomorrow?" · "What's the forecast for the weekend?" | The forecast |
| "What's the weather in Boston?" | Weather somewhere else |
| "Are there any weather alerts?" | Severe-weather alerts |

## Lights and plugs

Use the names shown on the **Devices** screen, or a room name.

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Turn on the kitchen light" · "Lamp off" ⚡ | Turns a device or a whole room on or off |
| "Lights out" · "All off" ⚡ | Turns everything off |
| "Dim the living room" · "Set the lamp to 40 percent" ⚡ | Changes brightness |
| "Is the porch light on?" ⚡ | Tells you |
| "Find new devices" | Looks for plugs and lights that aren't set up yet |

## Scenes and schedules

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Save this as movie time" ⚡ | Saves how the lights are right now as a scene |
| "Movie time" · "Activate movie time mode" · "Good night" ⚡ | Runs a scene you've saved under that name |
| "Turn on the porch light every day at sunset" | Creates a schedule |
| "What schedules are there?" · "Delete the porch light schedule" | Lists or removes schedules |
| "Turn on vacation mode" | Switches lights on and off at random while you're away |

## Shopping and to-do lists

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Add milk, eggs and bread to the shopping list" ⚡ | Adds items (you can name several) |
| "Take eggs off the shopping list" ⚡ | Removes an item |
| "What's on the shopping list?" · "What do we need from the store?" ⚡ | Reads the list |
| "Add call the plumber to the to-do list" ⚡ | Works with any list name |
| "Clear the shopping list" ⚡ | Empties it |

## Music and radio

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Play some jazz" · "Play WGBH" · "Play classic rock radio" ⚡ | Plays from your music collection, or finds a radio station |
| "Play the album Abbey Road" · "Play songs by Miles Davis" ⚡ | Plays from your music collection |
| "Play Yesterday by the Beatles" ⚡ | Plays a particular song |
| "Play Radiohead's latest album" · "Play the first album by R.E.M." ⚡ | Picks an album by date |
| "Next song" · "Previous song" ⚡ | Skips |
| "What's playing?" ⚡ | Tells you the song or station |
| "Stop the music" · "Stop" ⚡ | Stops |
| "Play jazz on the Sonos" · "Play on the Sonos" · "Play here" ⚡ | Sends music to a Bluetooth speaker, or back to the kitchen |
| "What speakers are there?" · "Pair a new speaker" ⚡ | Lists or pairs Bluetooth speakers |

Spotify works from the Spotify app on your phone: pick **Taco** as the
speaker.

## Cooking

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Find me a recipe for banana bread" · "Lasagna for 8 people" | Opens a recipe on screen, scaled to your servings |
| "Next step" · "Go back" · "Repeat that" ⚡ | Moves through the recipe |
| "Read me the ingredients" · "Go to step 4" · "Start over" ⚡ | More recipe navigation |
| "Close the recipe" · "I'm done cooking" ⚡ | Closes it |
| "How many tablespoons in a cup?" | Kitchen questions and conversions |

## Your day

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "What's my briefing?" · "Brief me" · "What's going on today?" ⚡ | Weather, alerts, your calendar, reminders and the shopping list |
| "What's on my calendar tomorrow?" | Calendar events |

## Anything else

Taco can answer general questions and search the web: "Who won the game last
night?", "What can I substitute for buttermilk?", "How long do I boil an egg?"

You can also change how Taco sounds: "What voices do you have?" and then
"Switch to the British voice".

## On the screen

- **Tap** anywhere to wake the screen from the clock screensaver or night mode.
- The **Devices**, **Lists** and **Settings** buttons are on the home screen.
- A tap on a ringing alarm stops it.
